2UnderstandThe WebGIS makes the evidence understandable without GIS software.

4DiscussThe school community reviews observations before proposing action.
5MonitorThe same area can be checked again after protection or greening activities.

ENVIRONMENTAL OUTCOME

What EcoTrack measures

EcoTrack monitors changes in vegetation spectral response and provides a structured way to verify local land conditions. It does not assume that every decrease is caused by tree removal. Field evidence is part of the method.

SOCIAL MOVEMENT OUTCOME

What participation changes

The school becomes a community environmental anchor: students move from passive awareness to collecting evidence, discussing local conditions, and monitoring whether community actions correspond to measurable environmental change.
